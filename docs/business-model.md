# Aivora – Business Model

## 1. Business Overview

Aivora is a B2B SaaS (Software as a Service) platform providing AI-powered automation and operational support for hotels and serviced apartments.

The business will generate recurring revenue by offering hospitality businesses access to AI tools, intelligent agents, integrations, and operational features through subscription plans.

## 2. Target Customers

### Primary Segment
- Independent hotels.
- Boutique hotels.
- Serviced apartments.
- Small and medium-sized hotel operators.

### Future Segments
- Hotel management companies.
- Multi-property hospitality groups.
- Large hotel operators.
- Hospitality service providers.

## 3. Customer Problems

Aivora addresses challenges including:

- Repetitive guest inquiries.
- Limited staff availability outside working hours.
- Manual handling of guest requests.
- Delays in communication between departments.
- Operational inefficiencies.
- Lack of consolidated operational insights.

## 4. Value Proposition

Aivora helps hospitality businesses:

- Improve guest response times.
- Reduce repetitive workloads.
- Provide multilingual guest support.
- Improve coordination between departments.
- Maintain more consistent guest communication.
- Gain visibility into guest requests and operational activity.

Aivora is designed to support hotel employees, not simply replace human interaction.

## 5. Revenue Streams

### 5.1 Monthly Subscriptions

Hotels pay a recurring subscription based on their selected plan, property size, usage, and enabled features.

### 5.2 Setup and Onboarding Fees

One-time fees may apply for:
- Initial configuration.
- Staff onboarding.
- Custom workflows.
- System integrations.

### 5.3 Premium AI Features

Additional revenue may come from:
- Advanced AI agents.
- Higher usage limits.
- Advanced analytics.
- Custom automation workflows.
- Additional communication channels.

### 5.4 Enterprise Solutions

Larger hotel groups may require:
- Multi-property management.
- Custom integrations.
- Dedicated support.
- Custom security and deployment requirements.

## 6. Proposed Subscription Structure

The following are initial pricing hypotheses, not confirmed market prices.

### Starter
For small hotels and serviced apartments.

- Core AI guest assistant.
- Basic guest inquiry automation.
- Standard reporting.
- Limited usage.

### Professional
For growing hospitality businesses.

- Multiple AI agents.
- Department request routing.
- Advanced workflows.
- Expanded integrations.
- Advanced reporting.

### Enterprise
For hotel groups and larger operators.

- Multi-property capabilities.
- Custom integrations.
- Advanced administration.
- Dedicated support.
- Custom deployment options where required.

Final pricing will be determined through customer interviews, competitor research, and pilot testing.

## 7. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 – UAE Pilot
- Identify independent hotels and serviced apartments.
- Interview hotel managers and operations teams.
- Validate the most important operational problems.
- Recruit pilot customers.
- Measure the value delivered.

### Phase 2 – UAE Commercial Launch
- Convert successful pilots into paying customers.
- Build partnerships with hospitality technology providers.
- Develop case studies.
- Introduce subscription plans.

### Phase 3 – GCC Expansion
- Expand into selected GCC markets.
- Support regional languages and requirements.
- Build partnerships with hotel operators.

## 8. Key Business Metrics

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
- Customer retention rate.
- Average revenue per property.
- AI usage cost per property.
- Guest response time.
- Automation completion rate.
- Customer satisfaction.

## 9. Main Cost Categories

- AI model and API usage.
- Cloud hosting and infrastructure.
- Software development.
- Third-party integrations.
- Customer support.
- Sales and marketing.
- Security and compliance.

## 10. Business Model Objective

Build a scalable recurring-revenue SaaS business that delivers measurable operational value to hospitality businesses.

## 11. Assumptions to Validate

- Hotels are willing to pay for AI-powered operational support.
- Guest inquiry automation is a high-priority use case.
- Integration with existing hotel systems is commercially feasible.
- The platform can deliver value while maintaining appropriate human oversight.
- AI usage and support costs allow sustainable subscription margins.
